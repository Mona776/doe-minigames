INPUT REFERENCE: the attached montage is an ACTUAL CURRENT DEVICE/SIMULATOR screenshot. ART DIRECTION:

Large clearly visible pixel blocks; every icon and glyph constructed of simple crisp square pixels. No anti-aliasing, no thin high-res detailed typography, no vector-smooth curves. Very limited color palette: near-black charcoal #15161C, panel #1F212A, grid #262935, muted moss #4A6B3A, main sage outline #9CC36B, lime #C9F25B, warm amber #F5C242, cream #EFE6CF, muted gray #9C968A, restrained red #E0474C; the dog is simple warm brown #C08552 with dark brown ears #8A5530, gray rocks. Flat fills, tiny 1px/2px bevel/shadow at most. A discrete 1px inset green screen edge; no plastic device mockup, no ornate casing. No glossy gradients, no ornamental flowers, no landscape illustration, no bright blue-white UI. Light scanline feel can be extremely subtle, never obscure pixel readability.

DELIVERABLE COMPOSITION:
A carefully typeset OFF-WHITE presentation board, 3 columns × 3 rows of NINE exact SQUARE screens, each representing a 240×240 full screen. The nine square screen views must have matching dimensions, no aspect-ratio stretching. Small accurate Chinese captions OUTSIDE each screen in the gutter. A compact component strip below the nine screens with individual button states, HUD pieces and tiny icons. Short board heading "贪吃腊肠 · 复古像素 UI". This must feel like a practical executable UI art specification board rather than a marketing illustration or a wireframe. Fully designed panels, no empty placeholders or dashed rectangles.

IN-GAME CONTEXT IN EVERY RELEVANT PANEL:
Retain a very quiet 10×10 grid occupying a full 200×200 pixel area, approximately x20 y30. Top HUD has just stage, bone-progress, best score. Keep it compact; do NOT steal grid space with a large decorative header or bottom toolbar during gameplay.
